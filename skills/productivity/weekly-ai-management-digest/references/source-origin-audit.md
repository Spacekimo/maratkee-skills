# Source-Origin Audit

Run this audit after sourcing and before final drafting. It checks that a recurring digest mixes curated-mail discovery with independent research.

## Record two fields for each substantive claim

- `origin_layer`: `newsletter` when a curated email or feed surfaced the claim or theme; `research` when it originated in extended or deep research.
- `evidence_url`: the canonical page supporting the final wording. A newsletter-origin claim can and should use primary evidence where available.

Do not classify by the domain of the final URL. The audit measures where editorial material originated; evidence validation remains a separate gate.

## Count substantive material

Count each distinct body claim, case, or bounded early signal that informs a management conclusion. Exclude reading-list-only links, duplicate citations, fact-check-only sources, and generic promotion.

## Gate

Target a 50/50 split. A small deviation requires a written reason; neither origin layer may exceed 60% of substantive material.

When rebalancing, add material that advances the current issue's distinct object. Do not pad with weak links or re-run a preceding issue's argument.

## Publication record

Save the audit beside the source register: counts, claim IDs, origin layer, discovery item, final evidence URL, and any deviation rationale.
